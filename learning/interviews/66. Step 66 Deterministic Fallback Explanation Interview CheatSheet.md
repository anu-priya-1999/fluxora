# Step 66: Deterministic Fallback Explanation — Interview Cheat Sheet

## 30-Second Pitch
"In Fluxora, the deterministic graph/impact/simulation engine is the source of truth, and the LLM is just a presentation layer. In Step 66, I implemented a pure deterministic fallback generator for impact explanations and simulation narrations. When the Model Gateway hits terminal unavailability/timeouts or structured-output validation fails after the 1-retry ceiling, the orchestrator synthesizes a schema-valid, evidence-cited JSON explanation directly from the trusted context—preserving truncation, unresolved-file, and synthetic-simulation caveats, abstaining when evidence is missing, and never masking auth or tenant errors."

## Key Questions & Answers

1. **Where does fallback live, and why not inside `ModelGateway` or the validator?**
   - It lives in `apps/workers/src/ai/fallback/` and is invoked by `orchestrateImpactExplanation` and `orchestrateSimulationNarration`. `ModelGateway` handles provider retries and secondary model fallback (`haiku`); the validator enforces strict schema/manifest compliance. Keeping deterministic fallback at the orchestrator boundary preserves separation of concerns and ensures fallback has access to the full trusted `ImpactExplanationContext` / `SimulationNarrationContext`.

2. **When does fallback trigger, and what errors must NEVER trigger it?**
   - Triggers on: `PROVIDER_UNAVAILABLE`, `TIMEOUT_ERROR`, `RATE_LIMIT_ERROR`, `RETRIES_EXHAUSTED`, `INVALID_RESPONSE`, or `validation_failed_after_retries`.
   - Never triggers on: tenant/ownership `not_found`, `not_completed`, `invalid_input`, `AUTHENTICATION_ERROR`, `CONFIGURATION_ERROR`, `ABORTED`, or programming errors.

3. **How do you prevent fallback from hallucinating or masking missing evidence?**
   - Every claim only cites IDs present in `context.allowedEvidenceIds`. Findings without verified evidence are excluded and counted in `caveats`. If `allowedEvidenceIds` is empty, fallback explicitly abstains (`claims: []`, `confidence: "low"`, `fallbackAbstained: true`).

4. **How do consumers distinguish AI-generated vs. fallback vs. unavailable?**
   - AI-generated: `status: "completed"`, `validation_status: "passed"`, `model_used: "claude-..."`.
   - Deterministic fallback: `status: "completed"`, `validation_status: "fallback"`, `model_used: null`, `metadata.deterministicFallback: true`.
   - Unavailable: `status: "failed"` or `"rejected"`, `output_text: null`, `output_structured: null`.

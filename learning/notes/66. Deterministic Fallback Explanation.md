# 66. Deterministic Fallback Explanation

## Core Idea

Fluxora's AI layer is a **presentation layer**, never the source of truth for graph, impact, or simulation facts (Principle P1/P10, ADR-010). When the LLM provider is unavailable, times out, or produces invalid/ungrounded output after bounded corrective retries, `impact_explanation` and `simulation_narration` fall back to a deterministic template generator over the already-assembled, tenant-scoped context.

## Key Design Decisions

1. **Terminal Failure Boundary Only**:
   - Fallback is invoked in the orchestrator *only* after `ModelGateway` retries/secondary-model fallback are exhausted or after `validate*WithRetry` exhausts its 2-attempt ceiling.
   - Context-loading errors (`not_found`, `not_completed`, `invalid_input`), tenant/ownership failures, `AUTHENTICATION_ERROR`, `CONFIGURATION_ERROR`, `ABORTED`, and programming errors **never** trigger fallback.
2. **Evidence Grounding & Abstention**:
   - Every fallback claim cites only IDs present in `context.allowedEvidenceIds`.
   - Findings/results lacking verified evidence are excluded and disclosed in `caveats`.
   - When `allowedEvidenceIds` is empty (or no findings have verified evidence), the fallback **explicitly abstains** (`claims: []`, `confidence: "low"`, `fallbackAbstained: true`) rather than inventing claims.
3. **Provenance**:
   - `status: "completed"`, `validationStatus: "fallback"`, `modelUsed: null`, `promptVersion: "*-fallback-v1"`.
   - Consumers can cleanly distinguish model-generated (`validationStatus: "passed"`), deterministic fallback (`validationStatus: "fallback"`), and unavailable (`status: "failed" | "rejected"`).

# 65. AI Evaluation Suite

## Why Manifest Membership Is Not Enough

Fluxora enforces three distinct grounding guarantees:
1. **Structural evidence association** (Steps 60/63/64): Evidence rows are linked to the exact `subjectId` and `analysisRunId` during context assembly.
2. **Evidence manifest membership** (Step 61): Every cited `evidence_id` in the LLM JSON output belongs to `allowedEvidenceIds`.
3. **Semantic evidence support** (Step 65): The cited evidence record actually supports the natural-language claim and does not contradict deterministic findings.

A model can pass (1) and (2) while still hallucinating—for example, by citing a valid `evidence_id` from an unrelated finding or misstating a simulation's `degraded` state as `failed`.

## Hallucination Rate & Ambiguous Claims

- `hallucinationRate = (unsupported + contradicted) / totalEvaluatedClaims`
- `ambiguous` claims are **never** silently classified as `supported`; they are tracked separately and included in `conservativeHallucinationRate`.
- The `<2%` hallucination ceiling is a **prompt-promotion target**, requiring at least 20 live evaluated claims (`minSampleSizeForStatisticalConfidence = 20`). Mock CI runs or undersized samples report `inconclusive` rather than a false `pass`.

## Mock vs. Live Execution

- `ci_mock` mode runs offline in CI on every commit to verify the evaluation harness, validators, entity checks, and adversarial containment.
- `live` mode is opt-in (`USE_REAL_LLM=true` + `ANTHROPIC_API_KEY`) with hard case, token, cost, and duration budgets.
